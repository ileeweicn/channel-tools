Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

channel-tools ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it connects to. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission through Google OAuth, the app accesses the owner's own YouTube channel data through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: channel and video details, and performance reports such as views, watch time and traffic sources. It may update the owner's own video titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to review and manage the owner's own channels. Reports are stored on the owner's own computer. Authorization tokens are stored locally with restricted file permissions and are never shared.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer data to third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Other users

The app is not offered to the public. It does not collect data about viewers or any other YouTube users beyond the aggregate reports YouTube provides to a channel owner.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue in this site's GitHub repository.
